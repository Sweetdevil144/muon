## Why this exists

PolyForm Noncommercial permits noncommercial purposes only. Read strictly, that
excludes an engineer who installs MUON and uses it while doing their job.
Excluding that person was never the intent, and it would be a strange thing to
forbid in a tool that runs on one machine, drives that person's own agent-CLI
logins, and sends nothing anywhere.

What is reserved is not the individual. It is **the company as a coordinated
whole** — many engineers' agents linked into one crew, sharing one brain. That
is the product MUON is building toward, and that is the product that is licensed.

## Definitions

- **You** — one natural person. Not a company, team, or other organization.
- **Your Devices** — computers you personally use and control, **including a
  laptop or workstation your employer or client issued to you**, provided you
  are the person operating it.
- **Your Instance** — a MUON installation on Your Devices, running under your
  own accounts and your own agent-CLI credentials.
- **Linked Operation** — two or more people's MUON instances sharing, syncing,
  or coordinating memory, code graphs, crews, claims, or findings, whether
  through a MUON-provided service, a self-hosted service, or any other means.
